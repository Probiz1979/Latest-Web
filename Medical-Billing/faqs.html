General & Outsourcing

Outsourcing your medical billing to a specialized partner like Probiz eliminates the hidden costs of an in-house team—salaries, benefits, turnover, and continuous training. Instead of relying on a small internal team that might struggle with the sheer volume of changing payer rules, you gain an entire department of certified coders and billing specialists. We utilize advanced rule engines and automated workflows to maximize first-pass acceptance rates and accelerate cash flow, allowing you to focus entirely on patient care.

Probiz supports a wide array of medical specialties including Cardiology, Orthopedics, Neurology, Behavioral Health, Dermatology, Gastroenterology, Oncology, Radiology, and General Practice. We assemble dedicated pods of coders tailored to your specific specialty so they thoroughly understand your unique CPT codes, modifiers, and payer-specific LCD/NCD policies.

Pricing & Contracts

Our pricing is primarily based on a percentage of net collections. This "pay-for-performance" model means we only get paid when you get paid, perfectly aligning our incentives with yours. The exact percentage depends on the size of your practice, your specialty, and your average monthly claim volume. Contact us for a custom proposal tailored to your specific needs.

Compliance & Security

Yes. We adhere to the strictest HIPAA and HITECH regulations. We sign a comprehensive Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with all clients. Our physical facilities feature biometric access controls, and our digital infrastructure utilizes end-to-end AES-256 encryption, zero-trust network access, and strict role-based data permissions.

Our coding team consists of certified professionals (AAPC and AHIMA). Furthermore, every claim runs through a comprehensive scrubbing engine that checks thousands of payer-specific edits before transmission. We also perform regular internal audits to guarantee our coding maintains industry-leading accuracy rates.

Because we operate within your existing EHR/PMS environment, your data remains safely stored on your own secure servers or your cloud provider's servers. We only access the data remotely via secure, encrypted VPN connections, ensuring we never download or store ePHI on unauthorized local machines.
