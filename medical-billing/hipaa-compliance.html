Security & HIPAA Compliance

Every piece of Protected Health Information that passes through our systems is treated as a non-negotiable trust. We don't merely comply with HIPAA — we engineer our entire operation around it.

Three Layers of Protection

HIPAA mandates three types of safeguards. We go beyond minimum standards in each category.

Administrative Safeguards

Policies, procedures, and workforce controls that govern how PHI is accessed and managed. We maintain a dedicated Privacy Officer, conduct documented risk assessments annually, and require all staff to complete HIPAA certification before accessing any patient data.

  • Annual Risk Assessment & Remediation
  • Dedicated Privacy & Security Officers
  • Contingency & Breach Response Plans
  • Signed BAAs with all PHI-touching vendors

Technical Safeguards

The technology architecture that controls electronic PHI access and transmission. We implement enterprise-grade security infrastructure that exceeds what most hospital systems deploy for their own data.

  • AES-256 Encryption at Rest & in Transit
  • Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) Required
  • Real-Time Audit Logging of All PHI Access
  • Zero-Trust Network Architecture

Physical Safeguards

The controls that protect the physical environments where PHI is processed and stored. Our facilities are access-controlled at multiple layers — from building perimeter to individual workstation level.

  • Biometric-Controlled Access to Work Floors
  • 24/7 CCTV Security Monitoring
  • Locked, Access-Controlled Server Rooms
  • NIST-Compliant Data Destruction Protocols

End-to-End Encryption

All PHI is encrypted using AES-256 standard — both when stored on our servers and during transmission to payers, clearinghouses, and providers. TLS 1.3 is enforced on all external connections.

Encrypted Backup Infrastructure

Redundant, geographically distributed encrypted backups ensure zero data loss and full disaster recovery capability with RPO <1 hour and RTO <4 hours.

Mobile Device Management

All mobile endpoints accessing PHI are enrolled in our MDM platform with full-disk encryption, remote wipe capability, and geofencing restrictions.

Secure Email & File Transfer

PHI is never transmitted via standard email. We use SFTP, encrypted email gateways, and secure patient portal APIs that meet 21 CFR Part 11 standards.

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Staff are granted the minimum necessary PHI access required for their specific role. A coder sees only the data needed for their queue — nothing more.

Multi-Factor Authentication

MFA is mandatory for all system access — no exceptions. Every login requires a second-factor verification through our authenticator platform.

24/7 Audit Log Monitoring

Every PHI access event is logged with user ID, timestamp, and action taken. Our security team reviews anomaly reports daily via automated SIEM alerts.

Automatic Session Termination

Workstations auto-lock after 5 minutes of inactivity. Sessions are terminated automatically after 15 minutes — preventing unauthorized access to unattended screens.

72-Hour Breach Detection Goal

Our SOC monitors for indicators of compromise around the clock. Our target detection-to-containment window is under Rapid — aligned with EU GDPR standards even for US operations.

HIPAA Breach Notification Compliance

In the event of any confirmed breach, we manage the full HIPAA notification workflow — provider notification within 60 days, HHS reporting, and media notice for large breaches (>500 individuals).

Business Associate Agreement (BAA)

Every client engagement begins with a signed BAA that clearly defines our security obligations, liability boundaries, and notification responsibilities as your Business Associate.

Documented Incident Response Plan

We maintain and regularly test a documented IRP with defined roles, communication trees, and escalation procedures — validated by annual tabletop exercises.

Pre-Employment HIPAA Certification

Every Probiz employee completes a comprehensive HIPAA certification program before handling any client data. Certification is renewed annually with an updated curriculum reflecting current threats.

Phishing Simulation Program

We conduct quarterly simulated phishing campaigns to test staff awareness. Any employee who clicks a simulated phishing link receives immediate remedial training.

PHI Handling Procedures

Clear written policies govern every scenario: what to do if a PHI document is accidentally emailed to the wrong address, how to handle a lost device, and how to report a suspected breach internally.

Annual Compliance Attestation

Every employee signs an annual compliance attestation confirming they have read, understood, and will adhere to all HIPAA and data security policies — creating a documented accountability trail.
