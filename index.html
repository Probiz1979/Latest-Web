A BPO Company That Actually Delivers

Probiz is a fully operational business process outsourcing company headquartered in Dubai. We handle the outsourced functions that businesses depend on to grow. This includes sales, customer support, back office administration, debt recovery, medical billing, HR, digital marketing, and more. Our clients range from local enterprises to major international corporations serving global markets.

Our team is multicultural by design. We recruit professionals who are fluent in English, Arabic, Urdu, Hindi, and many other languages because meaningful client interactions require cultural fluency alongside linguistic accuracy. Combined with over 25 years of leadership experience in BPO and enterprise operations, we are exceptionally well positioned to serve the complex needs of growing businesses across a wide range of industries.

We are not a startup figuring things out as we go. When a client comes to Probiz, we have the systems, the people, and the experience to get things moving quickly and professionally.
